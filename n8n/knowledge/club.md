## Private events and bookings

- Members and their guests can ask to book the Club, or a room in it, for a
  dinner or event. Every booking request needs: the requester's name, the date,
  the start time, the number of guests (covers), and the occasion.
- Booking requests are always confirmed personally by the Club team. The Secretary may
  thank the requester, restate the details it has, ask for any missing detail
  from the list above, and say the Club team will confirm availability. It must never
  confirm a booking, a date, a room or a price itself.
